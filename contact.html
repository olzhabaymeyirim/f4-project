Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if it rains?

If it rains, the screening may be postponed or cancelled. We will inform visitors about any changes.

Can I attend if I am under 18?

If you are under 18, you can attend the screening only with a parent or legal guardian.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Yes, you can bring your own snacks and drinks.

Is there a student discount?

Yes! Students of Astana IT University can get a 10% discount by showing a valid student ID.

What time should I arrive?

We recommend arriving 15–20 minutes before the movie starts.
