ABOUT US

It started with four girls and one idea.

We wanted to create a place where Astana could feel a little different at night.

Somewhere you could sit under the open sky, watch a movie with your friends, grab a drink and forget about the city for a couple of hours.

So we brought our love for cinema, our love for Astana and our own vision together.

Moon & Movies was born.
